# Technical Engineering Drawing Analysis & 3D CAD Modeling Specification

## 1. Part Classification & Purpose
* **Component Type:** **Machined Support Bracket / Bearing Block**
* **Primary Function:** Serves as a structural mounting bracket designed to house a cylindrical shaft, pin, or bearing within its upper vertical boss, while providing a ribbed/flanged base with a center cutout and mounting provisions to secure it to a mating flat surface.

---

## 2. Primary Datums & Coordinate System
To reconstruct this part accurately in a 3D CAD environment (e.g., SolidWorks, Creo, Fusion 360), use the following coordinate framework:
* **Origin (0, 0, 0):** Defined at the bottom-right-rear corner of the base (or aligned with the center axis of the upper circular boss for radial symmetry).
* **Primary Datum Plane ($XY$):** Bottom planar face of the base.
* **Secondary Datum Plane ($YZ$):** Rear vertical planar face of the base/flange.
* **Tertiary Datum Plane ($XZ$):** Center symmetry plane running longitudinally through the middle of the part.

### A. Base & Main Body Profile
* **Overall Length:** $100\text{ mm}$ (measured from rear vertical face to the center of the front semicircular cutout).
* **Overall Width (Span):** $62\text{ mm}$.
* **Base Height / Thickness:** $18\text{ mm}$ (vertical wall thickness at the sides).
* **Side Recesses (Top View):** 
  * Two symmetrical outer side channels of width $18\text{ mm}$ flanking the central body.
  * Flange wall thicknesses on left/right edges are $9\text{ mm}$ each.
* **Front Semicircular Cutout:** Defined by a radius of **$\text{R}22\text{ mm}$** centered on the front edge of the base profile.

### B. Upper Cylindrical Boss
* **Outer Diameter ($\varnothing$OD):** $\varnothing 44\text{ mm}$.
* **Inner Bore Diameter ($\varnothing$ID):** $\varnothing 28\text{ mm}$ (through-hole).
* **Vertical Height:** The center axis of the main boss is located at a height of **$50\text{ mm}$** from the bottom datum plane (base).
* **Transition Webs / Ribs:** Sloped structural ribs smoothly tangent from the top cylindrical boss down to the horizontal top surface of the base block.

### C. Internal Cavities, Slots & Bottom Cutouts
* **Bottom Rectangular Slot / Notch:**
  * **Width:** $28\text{ mm}$.
  * **Height (Depth from bottom base):** $9\text{ mm}$.
  * **Location from Rear Face:** $8\text{ mm}$ offset from the rear vertical wall.
